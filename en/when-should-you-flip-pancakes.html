When should you flip pancakes?

If you are asking when should you flip pancakes, the useful answer is a practical timing range plus the one source or warning sign that can change it. Food timing is about both quality and safety, so use practical ranges and discard anything that may be unsafe.

Flip pancakes when bubbles form on the surface, the edges look set, and the bottom is golden.
