When is bread dough ready?

If you are asking when is bread dough ready, the useful answer is a practical timing range plus the one source or warning sign that can change it. Food timing is about both quality and safety, so use practical ranges and discard anything that may be unsafe.

Short answer

Bread dough is ready for the next step when it has risen noticeably, feels airy, and slowly springs back when gently pressed.

Detailed answer

Use the short answer as a starting point, then adjust for your location, condition, device, recipe, schedule, or official source. When accuracy matters, check the current primary source rather than an old screenshot or copied post.

Practical notes

Keep a reminder, compare conditions, and re-check the source when timing can change. For food, health-like, safety, and official schedule questions, choose caution over convenience.

What to watch out for

Over-proofed dough may collapse or taste overly sour.
